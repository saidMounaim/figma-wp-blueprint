## 3. Pages

One line per page with its template: `Home - front-page.php, flexible sections`.

## 4. Sections (Flexible Content layouts)

One line per reusable section from Figma, with the pages that use it. `/figma`
fills this from the design.

## 5. Content types (Custom Post Types and taxonomies)

One line per CPT: name, archive and single pages needed, taxonomies, and key
fields. Write "none" if the site only has pages and posts.

## 6. Global settings

Header and footer content edited from the ACF options page (logo, menus,
contact info, socials, footer text, CTAs).

## 7. Interactions and animation

Menus, sliders, accordions, tabs, modals, filters, and any animation. Animation
details can be explored later; list them here so they are not forgotten.

## 8. Forms and integrations

Contact forms (which plugin, or a custom handler), newsletter, maps, analytics,
third-party embeds.

## 9. Plugins

Required plugins beyond ACF Pro. Keep the list short.

## 10. Deployment

Where the theme goes after local development (staging URL, host, how files are
uploaded). Deployment stays a manual step.
